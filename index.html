Meet Eric Gehnrich

A career spent winning B2B deals and building the teams that win them.

Eric Gehnrich is a fractional salesperson and fractional sales leader. He has spent his career in B2B sales for tech and tech-enabled solutions, across just about every corporate sales motion there is, including non-endemic partnerships within B2B2C channels.

He has led partnership sales and large deal responses, built and mentored sales territories, and designed the incentive plans that paid for them. He also still sells: his network reaches across the United States, and he uses it to generate real leads himself.

What sets him apart is the combination. Eric keeps unusually organized notes, processes, and internal systems, and he pairs that discipline with genuine personal outreach that connects the right parties at the right time.
